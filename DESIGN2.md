## Brand & Style

This design system expresses authoritative restraint, intellectual clarity, and institutional prestige. Rooted in the visual identity of Pacheco Espinoza & Cia. Abogados, the interface captures the ethos of high-stakes jurisprudence: measured, deliberate, unhurried, and resolute. 

The aesthetic is grounded in **Minimalist Legal Elegance**. It deliberately avoids decorative clutter, opting instead for generous breathing room, crisp editorial proportions, fine hairline structural rules, and a deep oceanic teal signature. The experience evokes the ambiance of an architecturally preserved legal chambers—white archival paper, bespoke binding, sharp ink, and silent authority.

Key pillars:
- **Judicious Restraint:** Every element serves an informational or procedural necessity.
- **Architectural Cadence:** Clean horizontal rules, generous line heights, and structured symmetry.
- **Dignified Contrast:** The tension between monumental deep teal (#0E5C6B), immaculate bright surfaces, and subtle warm stone parchment tones.

## Colors

The palette revolves around deep mineral teal, supported by subtle parchment grounds, deep slate charcoals, and a muted brass metallic accent reserved exclusively for legal milestones and distinctions.

- **Primary Teal (`#0E5C6B`):** The signature anchor. Represents integrity, institutional stability, and calm resolve. Used for primary navigation bars, primary CTA buttons, major legal case headers, and active state indicators.
- **Secondary Brass (`#D4AF37`):** An understated muted gold reminiscent of antique desk instruments and seal embossing. Used sparingly for badge accents, verified certifications, and premium service distinctions.
- **Tertiary Deep Slate (`#1A363D`):** An ultra-deep teal charcoal used for high-impact container panels, footers, and inverted callouts.
- **Neutral Noir (`#1E292B`):** Off-black charcoal for primary body text, ensuring rigorous legibility without the harshness of pitch black.
- **Background & Archival Surfaces:** Soft optic whites (`#FFFFFF`) layered over subtle stone white (`#F8F9FA`) and faint parchment gray (`#EFF2F3`) to emulate crisp bonded paper.

## Typography

Typography strikes an intentional dialogue between classic scholarship and modern administrative functionality.

- **Headline Font (EB Garamond):** Chosen for its pedigree in classical publishing, historical legal briefs, and humanistic authority. Used for titles, section headings, quote pullouts, and lawyer designations.
- **Body & Functional Font (Work Sans):** Chosen for its clean, geometric clarity, robust legibility in legal contracts, and neutral architectural demeanor. Handles data tables, case briefs, form controls, and technical copy with effortless precision.
- **Uppercase Kerning (All-Caps Subtitles):** Taking direct inspiration from the firm’s wordmark typography, category labels and institutional roles (`ABOGADOS`, `PRACTICE AREAS`) utilize `label-uppercase` with expanded tracking (`0.14em`) to establish a classic, statuesque presence.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a disciplined **Fixed Grid with Generous Marginal Space**. It respects the formal cadence of legal dossiers and executive publishing.

- **Grid Structure:** A 12-column desktop grid constrained within a `1240px` maximum reading container, framed by a minimum `2rem` viewport margin. Content retains disciplined horizontal and vertical axes.
- **Rhythm:** Spacing follows an 8pt architectural rhythm, favoring vertical spaciousness (`space-2xl` and `space-3xl`) between legal practices, partner bios, and article sections to avoid density anxiety.
- **Breakpoints:**
  - **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column grid; wide margins; multi-column dossier and split-screen layouts.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout; gutter scaled to `1.5rem`.
  - **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column layout; gutters compressed to `1rem`; primary headlines switch to mobile scales (`headline-lg-mobile`).

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids synthetic floating effects, plastic drop shadows, or heavy 3D skeuomorphism. Depth is established through **Low-Contrast Hairlines and Archival Tonal Layering**.

- **Hairline Rules & Dividers:** Visual separation is predominantly achieved using delicate 1px solid borders in `#0E5C6B` at 12% opacity (or `#E2E8F0` on neutral light cards).
- **Subtle Surface Tiers:** Backgrounds transition between pure white `#FFFFFF` for primary active documents, stone white `#F8F9FA` for page canvas, and `#EFF2F3` for inset data modules.
- **Ambient Focus Lift:** When interactive elevation is required (such as on client portal cards or partner directories), cards employ an ultra-subtle, non-directional tinted shadow:
  - `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(14, 92, 107, 0.06), 0 1px 3px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04);`
- **Focus Rings:** Accessible, sharp 2px solid offset ring in `#0E5C6B` with a 2px pure white clearance offset.

## Shapes

The shape language reflects crisp stationery and architectural structure. Forms are conservative, sharp, and softly tailored.

- **Curvature Hierarchy:** Level `1` (Soft). Default corner radius is `0.25rem` (4px). Structural cards and panels remain crisp, subtly softened just enough to prevent harshness on digital displays.
- **Containers & Modals:** Standard corners at `0.25rem` to preserve the presence of physical court filings and certificate parchment.
- **Circular Indicators:** Avatars, seal emblems, and status indicators strictly employ full circles (`50%`), mirroring the circular pen-and-column emblem of the firm's hallmark.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid deep teal (`#0E5C6B`) background, crisp white typography (`#FFFFFF`), `0.25rem` border radius, high-density padding (`12px 24px`). Hover state darkens gently to `#09404B` with a subtle 1px border shift.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Transparent background with a refined 1.5px border in `#0E5C6B`, text in `#0E5C6B`. Hover fills with `#0E5C6B` at 6% tint.
- **Text Link Button:** EB Garamond or Work Sans with an explicit hairline underline offset by 4px, transitioning to primary teal.

### Input Fields & Selects
- Framed in a single 1px hairline border (`#D0D7D9`).
- Crisp white background, transitioning on focus to an active `#0E5C6B` border paired with an ambient teal glow tint.
- Labels are positioned outside the input field in `label-uppercase` tracking for unmistakable clarity.

### Cards & Dossiers
- Background in pure `#FFFFFF` resting upon `#F8F9FA`.
- Bound with a 1px solid border (`rgba(14, 92, 107, 0.1)`).
- Card headers optionally include a 2px top accent border in solid `#0E5C6B` to signify legal authority.

### Chips & Badges
- Compact padding (`4px 10px`) with `0.25rem` radius.
- Practice area tags use `#0E5C6B` background at 8% opacity with dark teal text.
- Special verified accreditation chips feature a subtle secondary brass border (`#D4AF37`) with dark slate typography.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Square with 2px radius (checkbox) and clean circular frame (radio).
- 1.5px border in `#0E5C6B`. When checked, fully fills with `#0E5C6B` containing a crisp white geometric check mark.

### Legal Tables & Clause Lists
- Stripped of heavy vertical grids.
- Separated entirely by horizontal hairline rules in `#E5E9EA`.
- Row hover states engage a gentle `#F4F7F7` wash with immediate responsiveness.
