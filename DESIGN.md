## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an architectural, high-trust digital presence for legal counsel. The aesthetic merges corporate modern rigor with authoritative editorial refinement. Grounded in institutional competence, clarity, and discreet luxury, it evokes security, intellectual precision, and immediate accessibility for clients facing high-stakes legal situations.

Key visual pillars:
- **Structural Dividers:** Thin, vertical and horizontal geometric separation lines inspired directly by classic legal stationery and identity lockups.
- **Editorial Balance:** High-contrast pairing of an authoritative serif for key titular moments and legal practice headlines against a pristine, neutral sans-serif for rigorous operational legibility.
- **Controlled Accents:** Deep petroleum forest tones provide institutional grounding, punctuated purposefully with sharp emerald signals for high-priority client intake and direct communication channels.

## Colors

The color palette is derived from deep petroleum green and corporate slate, balanced by a sharp emerald operational green.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#144A42`):** Corporate petroleum green. Used for headers, prominent surface fills, high-level structural containers, and primary brand indicators. Dark variant `#0F3D36` anchors active states and dark container sections; light tint `#1D6156` serves for selected highlights and subtle hover overlays.
- **Secondary (`#4A5351`):** Corporate slate. Governs secondary typographic hierarchy, technical metadata, subtitles, and standard iconography.
- **Tertiary / Accent (`#0DBA83`):** Radiant emerald. Exclusively reserved for conversion moments: instant WhatsApp contact, appointment scheduling triggers, and primary actionable states. Deep variant `#0FA976` provides hover feedback.
- **Neutral Dark (`#1E2423`):** Deep charcoal-tinted ink. Used for body text to maintain optimal contrast without the visual harshness of pure black.
- **Neutral Light & Surfaces:** Clean pure white (`#FFFFFF`) serves as the base canvas, paired with `#F7F9F8` for alternating structural card bands and nested legal modules. Border lines and dividers utilize crisp, muted slate (`#D8DCDA`).

## Typography

The typographic system utilizes an authoritative dialogue between **Playfair Display** and **Outfit**.

- **Editorial Headings (Playfair Display):** Conveys the weight, precedent, and distinction of institutional jurisprudence. Reserved for primary case headers, firm mottos, practice area titles, and partner credentials.
- **Functional Interface (Outfit):** Provides a clean, modern humanist structure for case briefs, statutory readings, legal service breakdowns, and high-density data tables.
- **Overline / Category Trackers (`label-md`):** Set in full uppercase with expanded letter-spacing (`0.12em`) to mirror luxury legal letterheads and corporate filing indices.

## Layout & Spacing

A structured 12-column grid system anchors the presentation of institutional services and attorney dossiers.

### Breakpoints & Flow
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns, 32px gutters, maximum container width of `75rem` (`1200px`) centered. Asymmetric column spans (e.g., 5-column overview alongside 7-column case intake or staff profile) replicate physical legal briefing layouts.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8 columns, 24px gutters, 32px outer safe margins. Multi-column partner cards collapse to a clean 2x2 matrix.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 16px gutters, 16px outer safe margins. Horizontal structural line dividers convert seamlessly to horizontal divider separators.

### Vertical Rhythm
Section transitions use expansive spacing (`space-3xl` to `space-4xl`), creating room for considered reading and deliberation. Card interiors apply uniform padding (`space-lg` to `space-xl`) paired with 1px hairline perimeter borders.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids heavy drop shadows in favor of crisp architectural precision, utilizing tonal layers and low-contrast perimeter outlines.

- **Flat Tonal Hierarchy:** Depth is created through surface contrast: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) against Off-White/Soft Slate (`#F7F9F8`), bounded by crisp 1px borders (`#D8DCDA`).
- **Ambient Focus Elevation:** When interaction demands elevation (e.g., floating legal consultation trigger, active modality, attorney card hover):
  - `elevation-subtle`: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(20, 74, 66, 0.06), 0 1px 3px -1px rgba(20, 74, 66, 0.04)`
  - `elevation-hover`: `0 12px 24px -6px rgba(20, 74, 66, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(20, 74, 66, 0.03)`
  - `elevation-modal`: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(15, 61, 54, 0.16), 0 0 0 1px rgba(216, 220, 218, 0.6)`
- **Architectural Dividers:** Vertical line elements styled directly after the firm's brand identity (`#144A42` or `#D8DCDA` at 1px width) are deployed to segment contact information, practice group tags, and credential badges.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a **Soft (Level 1)** geometry, communicating institutional stability and order.

- **Containers & Cards:** `0.25rem` (`4px`) default corner radius, maintaining disciplined edges that align with formal corporate stationery.
- **Interactive Buttons & Inputs:** `0.25rem` (`4px`) to preserve structural weight.
- **Pill Exceptions:** High-urgency contextual badges, practice tags, and the floating WhatsApp quick-contact trigger utilize fully rounded pill caps (`9999px`) to contrast cleanly against geometric content grids.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA (Emerald Action):** Solid `#0DBA83` fill, `#FFFFFF` text, `0.25rem` corner radius, `label-lg` typography. Hover transitions to `#0FA976`.
- **Institutional / Corporate Button:** Solid `#144A42` background with `#FFFFFF` text. Hover transitions to `#0F3D36`.
- **Secondary / Ghost Button:** Transparent background, 1px solid `#144A42`, `#144A42` text. Hover fills `#144A42` at 5% opacity.
- **WhatsApp Quick Contact:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), `#0DBA83` solid fill with integrated iconography, white typography, accompanied by floating subtle elevation.

### Cards & Legal Dossiers
- **Practice Area & Attorney Cards:** Structured containers built on `#FFFFFF` or `#F7F9F8` with a 1px `#D8DCDA` outline. Features a deliberate vertical divider rule (`#144A42`) setting off credentials or case metrics. Hover states introduce `elevation-hover` with a 1px border shift to `#144A42`.

### Inputs & Contact Fields
- **Form Controls:** Clean input fields with `#F7F9F8` background, 1px `#D8DCDA` border, `0.25rem` border radius, and `body-md` typography. On focus: `#FFFFFF` fill with 1px `#144A42` border and zero outer halo glare.

### Chips & Practice Group Badges
- **Status & Area Tags:** Bordered chips with `0.75rem` padding, subtle `#F7F9F8` fill, 1px `#D8DCDA` border, and `label-md` tracking.

### Structural Dividers & Vertical Hairlines
- **Stationery Dividers:** 1px rules in `#D8DCDA` (or `#144A42` when separating brand partners), maintaining a minimum 24px clearance to establish disciplined layout pauses.
